The Problem

Nature heals. But insurance needs proof.

Nature-based therapy is clinically effective — but without objective, real-time physiological data, payers categorize it as wellness, not medicine. Clinicians doing their most meaningful work in the field are left without the documentation to justify it.

Eco-Sync AI closes that gap. It synchronizes wearable biosensors with a clinician dashboard, detecting stress events the moment they occur in a natural environment — and generating the evidence record that changes how this work is classified, funded, and accessed.

The Platform

Four systems. One closed loop.

From wearable sensor to insurance submission — every step handled with clinical precision.

📡
Biometric Capture

HRV and EDA collected continuously from weatherproof wearables. Real-time data streamed to the clinician the moment a stress event occurs.

🧠
AI Analysis

A machine learning engine trained on both ABA and MFT datasets detects deviations from each patient's unique Window of Tolerance.

🥽
Clinical Dashboard

Real-time physiological overlay via AR glasses or mobile. The therapist maintains eye contact while viewing a live vitals feed and grounding prompts.

📋
Evidence Engine

Post-session, the system compiles biometric data into a Biometric Impact Report — defensible documentation for insurance reimbursement.
